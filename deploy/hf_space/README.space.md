ScrapTrust open-testing build. Open the app at the direct link (`https://<owner>-<space>.hf.space`), not the
Hugging Face page, because the app refuses to be shown inside another site's frame.

Test data only: whatever you type or upload is visible to the other testers on the Records page, and it is wiped when the
Space restarts. Composition numbers labelled INFERRED are model estimates with a 90 % band; OBSERVED values are instrument
readings; UNKNOWN means the value can't be supported.
